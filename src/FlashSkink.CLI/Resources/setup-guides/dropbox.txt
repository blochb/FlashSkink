Dropbox — BYOC Setup Guide
==========================

Before running `{cli} setup add --provider dropbox` you need to create your own
Dropbox app. FlashSkink is a "Bring Your Own Cloud" tool: it ships no shared
credentials, so each user creates a private app in their own Dropbox account.

Step 1 — Create a Dropbox app
  1. Go to https://www.dropbox.com/developers/apps
  2. Click "Create app".
  3. Choose "Scoped access" and "Full Dropbox" (or "App folder" for isolation).
  4. Give the app a name and click "Create app".

Step 2 — Configure permissions
  1. Go to the Permissions tab of your new app.
  2. Enable: files.content.write, files.content.read, account_info.read.
  3. Click "Submit" to save.

Step 3 — Note your credentials
  1. On the Settings tab, copy the App key (Client ID) and App secret (Client Secret).

Step 4 — Add a redirect URI
  1. On the Settings tab, under "OAuth 2 > Redirect URIs", add this EXACT value
     (Dropbox matches the redirect URI exactly, including the port and path):
       http://127.0.0.1:53682/oauth-callback/

Step 5 — Add the tail
  Run:
    {cli} setup add --provider dropbox \
      --client-id <YOUR_APP_KEY> \
      --client-secret <YOUR_APP_SECRET> \
      --skink /path/to/your/skink

  Your browser will open for sign-in. Return to the terminal after approving.

Your client secret never leaves your machine unencrypted.
